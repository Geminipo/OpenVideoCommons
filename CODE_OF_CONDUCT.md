# Code of Conduct

OpenVideoCommons is a global open lab. We want contributors to feel welcome while keeping the project rigorous, safe, and evidence-driven.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but require reproducible evidence for technical claims.
- Welcome new contributors and help them make small, useful contributions.
- Credit people for accepted work.
- Keep discussions focused on open video AI, benchmarks, data governance, safety, and reproducible engineering.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Uploading private, sensitive, illegal, or copyright-unclear video data.
- Promoting deepfake abuse, non-consensual likeness use, or evasion of safety rules.
- Presenting unverifiable benchmark or compute claims as accepted results.
- Token, mining, or speculative reward promotion inside project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or ban participants who violate these expectations. Decisions should be documented when doing so does not create additional safety or privacy risk.
